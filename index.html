EMAM Shorts Poster

EMAM Shorts Poster is a video scheduling app. It takes short vertical clips that you made from your own videos and publishes them, with the title and description you wrote, to your own YouTube channel as YouTube Shorts, and to your own TikTok account, Instagram account and Facebook Page, at the times you choose.

It is built for EMAM: short clips from the public Q&A sessions of Engineer Muhammad Ali Mirza, posted regularly to the channel's social accounts.

What the app does

  1. Prepare clips. On your computer, you cut a long video into short vertical clips (up to 3 minutes each) and give each one a title, a description and hashtags.
  2. Schedule. You pick which clips to post, on which platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook) and at what dates and times.
  3. Upload and publish. At each scheduled time the app uploads the clip to your YouTube channel as a public Short, with your title and description, sends it to TikTok (as a draft in your TikTok inbox, or as a post), and publishes it as a Reel on Instagram and Facebook.
  4. Report back. The app shows you the link of each published video, or the reason if a post failed, so you can retry it.

The app only posts videos you chose to post. It does not read, edit or delete any other videos, comments, messages or data on your accounts.

Why the app asks for access to your Google account

When you click Connect YouTube, Google asks you to allow two permissions. The app uses each one for a single, specific reason:

  • Upload videos to your YouTube channel (youtube.upload): needed to upload your scheduled clips as YouTube Shorts and set their title, description, tags and visibility. This is the app's main feature.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): used only to read your channel's name, so the app can show you which channel the clips will be posted to before anything is uploaded.

The app does not use your Google data for advertising, does not sell or share it, and keeps only the sign-in token it needs to post on your behalf. You can remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account settings. All details are in the Privacy Policy.

Why the app asks for access to your TikTok account

When you connect TikTok, TikTok asks you to allow these permissions. The app uses each one for a single reason:

  • Read your profile info (user.info.basic): shows your TikTok username, so you can confirm the right account is connected, and builds the link to each post.
  • Upload videos as drafts (video.upload): sends your scheduled clip to your TikTok inbox, where you review it and post it yourself.
  • Post videos (video.publish): publishes your scheduled clip with your caption, if you choose direct posting.

The app reads nothing else from TikTok: no followers, messages, comments, likes or analytics.

Instagram and Facebook

For Instagram and Facebook, the app uses Meta's official API with your permission, only to publish your scheduled clips as Reels on your own Instagram professional account and Facebook Page.
