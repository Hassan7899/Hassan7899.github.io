EMAM Shorts Poster

EMAM Shorts Poster is a small private tool. It cuts short clips from the owner's own public Q&A lecture videos and posts them on a schedule to the owner's own Instagram account, Facebook Page and YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Uploads short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts) to the owner's YouTube channel, with a title and description.
  • Publishes the same clips as Reels on the owner's Instagram account and Facebook Page.
  • Runs on a schedule, so posts go out at set times.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the channel and accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account or data.

Google / YouTube access

The tool asks Google for permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (youtube.upload) and to read the channel's name (youtube.readonly). It is used for nothing else. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
